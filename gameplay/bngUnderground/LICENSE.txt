## Installed BeamNG content

Generated mission descriptors retain portions of the installed mission settings. Cosmetic/chassis/wheel/tyre/engine-support assignments, native mounting declarations, rim geometry, part-name translation metadata, preset performance figures and tuning defaults/limits are derived from installed vehicle configurations and JBeam data. Mandatory mission-local route/camera JSON data is generated from installed source files without changing the parsed data. Those portions retain BeamNG's original applicable content terms; the MIT grant above applies only to original work. Refer to the game's distributed licences and, for source marked accordingly, [bCDDL 1.1](https://www.beamng.com/bCDDL-1.1.txt). `gameplay/bngUnderground/nativeAssets.json` records original/generated paths and hashes for included native files. Tuning controls and component/engine identity/mount evidence identify their source archive/file/part/slot, donor configuration and relevant hashes in `vehicles.json`.

Vehicle models, JBeam/physics files, previews, sounds, flowgraphs, prefabs, optional route assets and constructors are referenced in the user's licensed installation. Raw installed assets included in the ZIP are limited to the small mandatory route/camera JSON files identified by provenance; vehicle catalogs additionally contain derived part/slot/tuning metadata. No third-party mods were installed or included. Development test dependencies stay outside the shipped ZIP and retain their own licences.
